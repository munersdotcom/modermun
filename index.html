The Conference

About MODERMUN

Our main goal is to offer the youth an incomparable opportunity to discover the world of international relations and the work of the United Nations. MODERMUN is about academic excellence.

MODERMUN takes place in a prestigious educational institution in our capital Ankara. The delegates will be able to engage in a simulation, where they will be able to put themselves in the place of the world's leaders, make decisions that will shape the future of our planet, suggest resolutions, learn how to find a middle ground between the interests of their country and the good of the whole world.
